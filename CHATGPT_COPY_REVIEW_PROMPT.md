# Copy and sales review prompt for ChatGPT

Copy everything below the line into ChatGPT. If your ChatGPT can browse the web, it can also open the live pages; the full text is included either way.

---

Act as two people at once: a world-class direct-response copywriter (think the clarity of David Ogilvy, the offer thinking of Alex Hormozi, the empathy of Joanna Wiebe) and a top-performing salesperson who sells digital services to small business owners in Ireland. Be honest and specific. I want to make more sales, not to hear that it's fine.

## My business

The Method Co. is run by Victor Miranda in Dublin. I build websites, apps and AI products, and I sell two ready-made offers to small Irish service businesses (cleaners, trades, beauty salons, trainers, restaurants, accountants):

1. **Instant Pricing Tool** (https://calculators.themethodco.co): a price calculator on the customer's own website. Their customers see a price instantly, then send the enquiry to the business on WhatsApp with every detail filled in. Launch price €150 (normally €299), one-off, first 5 businesses, free preview before paying.
2. **High-Conversion Landing Pages** (https://themethodco.co/landing-pages/): a single landing page for one offer or campaign, from €250, with 5 live demo pages. Preview before paying.

Both are promoted from my main site (https://themethodco.co), which is mainly a portfolio of products I've built.

My buyers are busy owners who aren't technical, often find me through Instagram, WhatsApp or word of mouth, and are price-sensitive but will pay for something that clearly brings them customers.

## What I want from you

Review everything below and give me:

1. **Verdict in 3 lines:** would this sell? Score each page 1 to 10 for clarity, persuasion and trust, and say why.
2. **The 5 biggest problems**, ranked by how many sales they cost, each with the exact fix (rewritten text, not advice).
3. **Headline and hero:** is it clear within 5 seconds what this is, who it's for and why they should care? Give me 5 alternative headlines and subheadlines for each page, and say which you'd test first.
4. **The offer:** is the price, scope and risk reversal (free preview, pay after approval) presented in the most compelling way? Are €150 and €250 priced right for this market? Suggest any bonuses, guarantees or packaging that would raise conversions without discounting further.
5. **Objections:** list the top objections an Irish small business owner would have that the page does not answer yet, and write the copy that answers each.
6. **Trust:** there are no testimonials or case studies yet (I won't fake them). What honest trust-builders can I add right now?
7. **Calls to action:** are the buttons and form the right asks? Is the form too long or too short? Rewrite the CTA text.
8. **Tone:** does it sound natural and Irish-friendly, or generic, salesy or AI-written? Point to the exact lines that feel off and rewrite them.
9. **Cross-selling:** how should the two offers and the main portfolio site point to each other so a visitor buys one and then the other?
10. **Quick wins:** 10 changes I can make this week, in order of impact.

Rules for your answer: be blunt, quote the exact text you're changing, give rewritten copy ready to paste, use British/Irish English, no em dashes, and never suggest fake reviews, fake scarcity or invented statistics.
