You are a senior performance marketer, direct-response copywriter and art director who has spent years running profitable Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) for small service businesses in Ireland and the UK. Think like a smart salesperson: every ad must stop the scroll, make one clear promise, and give the viewer one reason to click now. Then create the ads.

## The business

**The Method Co.** is run by Victor Miranda in Dublin, Ireland. He builds websites and digital tools for small Irish service businesses. Brand identity:
- Logo: a geometric "M" (left half white or navy, right half green) next to "THE METHOD CO." in bold uppercase with a green full stop. The logo files are attached; use them, never redraw them.
- Colours: navy `#0A0F14`, surface `#111827`, green `#22D87A` (primary, used for buttons and highlights), dark green `#0F8A54`, white `#FFFFFF`, light grey `#F9FAFB`.
- Font: Inter (bold or extra-bold for headlines, regular for body).
- Style: modern, clean, premium, confident. Lots of space, high contrast, one strong idea per image. Not cartoonish, not cheap-looking, no generic stock-photo smiles.

## The two products to advertise

### Product 1: Instant Pricing Tool
- What it is: a price calculator added to the business's own website. Their customers pick options (size, extras, frequency) and see an instant price estimate. One tap sends the enquiry to the business on WhatsApp with every detail filled in.
- Promise: "Give your customers an instant price estimate. Capture more enquiries automatically."
- Problem it solves: most people won't ring for a quote, they go to the competitor that shows a price; owners answer "how much?" ten times a day; leads arrive with no details; people ghost after hearing the price.
- Offer: launch price **€150** (normally **€299**), one-off, no monthly fees, for the first 5 businesses, until 30 November. A free working preview is built before they pay anything. Works on Wix, WordPress, Squarespace, Shopify or any site.
- Best for: cleaners, solar installers, builders and trades, accountants, personal trainers, any business that gets asked "how much?".
- Landing page: https://calculators.themethodco.co
- Main CTA: get a free preview (on WhatsApp).

### Product 2: High-Conversion Landing Pages
- What it is: one fast, beautiful landing page built around one offer or campaign, designed to turn visitors from ads and social media into enquiries, bookings and sales.
- Promise: "Your business deserves more than just clicks."
- Problem it solves: owners pay for ads and posts, send people to a slow or confusing homepage, and visitors leave without getting in touch.
- Offer: from **€250** per page. Includes copywriting, phone-first design in their brand, an enquiry form to their email plus WhatsApp or call buttons, speed and SEO basics, publishing on their domain, and 2 rounds of changes. They see a preview before paying. No monthly fee to us.
- Proof available: 5 live demo pages (cleaning company, beauty clinic, renovation company, personal trainer, restaurant event) that viewers can open and try.
- Best for: beauty and aesthetics, trades, fitness, hospitality and any business running a promotion or event.
- Landing page: https://themethodco.co/landing-pages/
- Main CTA: request a landing page, or see the 5 live demos.

## Target audience
Small business owners in Ireland (mainly Dublin, Cork, Galway and Limerick), aged 25 to 55, running a service business with 1 to 15 people. Busy, practical, not technical, sceptical of agencies and "marketing people", price-conscious but happy to pay for anything that clearly brings customers. They scroll Instagram and Facebook in the evening and between jobs. They use WhatsApp for everything.

## Hard rules (Meta policy and my brand)
- Never invent testimonials, reviews, client names, results, percentages or statistics. There are no case studies yet.
- No fake urgency. The only real deadline is the €150 launch price: first 5 businesses, until 30 November.
- Don't call out personal attributes ("Are you struggling...?", "You're losing money..."). Meta rejects that. Talk about the business situation instead ("Most people won't ring for a quote").
- No before/after money claims, no "guaranteed" results, no income promises.
- Keep text on the image short: a headline of up to about 8 words plus at most one short line. The rest goes in the primary text.
- British/Irish English (colour, enquiry, organise). Use € prices. No em dashes. No emoji overload (one or two at most, in primary text only).
- Every visual must be readable on a phone screen in under 2 seconds.

## What I want you to deliver

### 1. Strategy first (short)
- The 3 strongest selling angles for each product (for example: pain, outcome, speed or price, curiosity, "see it working"), and which you'd test first and why.
- Which product to put more budget behind first, and why.

### 2. Ten ad concepts: 5 per product
For each concept give me:
1. **Concept name and angle**
2. **Visual description**: exactly what is in the image, the layout, the colours (using the brand palette), where the logo goes, and the mood. Good ideas: a phone mockup showing a live price and a WhatsApp enquiry; a WhatsApp chat bubble with a booking request; a laptop and phone showing one of the demo landing pages; bold typographic posters on navy with a green highlight; a split comparison ("a quote form nobody fills in" versus "an instant price"); a crossed-out €299 with €150 big in green.
3. **Text on the image**: the headline, and an optional line under it.
4. **Primary text**: the copy above the ad, in 3 versions (short at 1 to 2 lines, medium, and long story-style). Hook in the first line.
5. **Headline** (under the image, max 40 characters) and **description** (max 30 characters).
6. **CTA button**: choose from Meta's options (Learn More, Get Quote, Send WhatsApp Message, Sign Up, Book Now).
7. **Where it should send people**: which page or section, or WhatsApp.

### 3. Generate the images
Create the images for the best 6 concepts (3 per product), using the attached logo and the brand colours:
- Each one in **1:1 (1080 × 1080)** for the feed and **9:16 (1080 × 1920)** for Stories and Reels, keeping the important content inside the centre safe area (leave the top and bottom 14% clear on 9:16).
- Also give a **4:5 (1080 × 1350)** version of the top 2.
- Make sure all text in the image is spelled correctly, the prices are exactly right (€150, €299, €250), and the logo is not distorted. If you can't place the real logo reliably, leave a clean empty space for it and tell me where, so I can add it in Canva.

### 4. Targeting and test plan (for a small budget, about €10 to €20 a day)
- Suggested audiences: location, ages, interests, and whether to use Advantage+ audience.
- How to structure the campaign: objective (Leads or Traffic, or messages to WhatsApp for the Pricing Tool), ad sets and how many ads each.
- What to measure, when to switch off a losing ad, and how to scale a winner.
- 3 retargeting ads for people who visited the landing pages but didn't enquire.

### 5. Quick review
End with a checklist I can use before publishing each ad: Meta policy, spelling, prices, the logo, mobile readability and the link.

Ask me no questions first; make smart assumptions and state them. Then deliver everything above.
