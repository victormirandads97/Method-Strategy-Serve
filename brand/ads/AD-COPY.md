# The Method Co. Meta ads: images and copy

Every image comes in three sizes:
- `-1x1.png` (1080 × 1080): Facebook and Instagram feed
- `-4x5.png` (1080 × 1350): feed, takes up more of the screen (recommended)
- `-9x16.png` (1080 × 1920): Stories and Reels

Rebuild them with `node script/ads/render-ads.mjs`.

## How to set it up (small budget, €10 to €20 a day)

1. **Two campaigns, one per product.** Instant Pricing Tool: objective "Engagement" with WhatsApp messages as the destination, or "Traffic" to the site. Landing pages: objective "Traffic" or "Leads".
2. **Audience:** Ireland (or Dublin, Cork and Galway if the budget is small), ages 25 to 55, Advantage+ audience on. Optional interests: Small business, Entrepreneurship, Facebook for Business.
3. **Put all three ads for each product in one ad set**, with the 4:5 image for the feed and 9:16 for Stories and Reels.
4. **Wait 3 to 4 days** before judging. Switch off any ad with a click-through rate below 0.8% or the highest cost per click. Move the money to the winner.
5. **Retargeting:** after a week, create an audience of people who visited the pages and show them ad 3 (the €150 offer) or ad 6 (the demos).

## Before you publish each ad

- The price is right (€150, €299, €250)
- The link opens the right page
- The image is the right size for where it's shown
- The deadline (30 November) is still in the future. After that, stop ad 3 and change "€150 launch price" to €299 in the other pricing tool ads.
