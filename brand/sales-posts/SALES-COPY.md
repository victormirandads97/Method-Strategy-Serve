## Posting tips

- **Carousel posts** do the selling: post one per offer, then pin the best one to your Instagram profile.
- **Hook ads**: put the 3 hooks for one offer in the same ad set and let Meta show the best one. After 3 to 4 days, switch off the weakest.
- **Stories**: use `hook-1-story.png` with a link sticker or "Send message" button.
- **Comment and DM keywords** (PREVIEW, WHATSAPP, PACK) make it easy to see which post brought the lead.
