// THE DIAGNOSTIC

Five questions.
One honest answer.

Run this on your role. The guide walks you through each one with context and scoring.

01

Does your work require judgment calls that change based on context you cannot document in advance?

Pattern-based tasks with fixed outputs are already automatable.

02

Do people pay for your presence, not just your output?

Relationships and trust transfer slower than tasks.

03

Is your value in doing the work or in knowing what work matters?

Execution is being commoditised. Curation and direction are not, yet.

04

Can you describe your role in a way that changes significantly based on who you are talking to?

Nuance and adaptability are harder to replicate than process.

05

Does your work become more valuable the longer you do it, in ways a new hire with the same tools cannot match?

Accumulated context compounds. Generic skill sets do not.
