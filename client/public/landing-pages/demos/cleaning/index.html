Questions

Before you book

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients leave a key or a code with us. Keys are labelled with a reference, never your address, and returned whenever you ask.

Do you bring your own products?

Yes. Products and equipment are included. If you prefer we use your own, or have surfaces that need something specific, just tell us when you book.

Can I change or skip a visit?

Yes, with 48 hours' notice. Changes inside that window may be charged, and we'll always tell you before we do.

What if something isn't right?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and put it right at no cost.
