The method

Twelve weeks.
Three phases.

No random workouts. Each phase builds on the last, and we review your training log together before moving on.

Weeks 1 to 4

Foundation

Technique, baseline lifts and a routine that fits your calendar.

Weeks 5 to 8

Build

Progressive loading and conditioning, adjusted to how you recover.

Weeks 9 to 12

Perform

Test your lifts, review the log and set the next block with confidence.

Who it's for

Honest about fit.

A good fit

  • You can train two to four times a week
  • You want structure, not just motivation
  • You're happy to log your sessions
  • You want strength that lasts

Not for you if

  • You want a quick fix before a holiday
  • You won't follow a written plan
  • You need medical rehabilitation (see a physio first)
