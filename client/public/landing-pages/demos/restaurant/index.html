The evening

One long table. Seven slow courses.

The Winter Table is our once-a-year tasting menu, cooked from what the west coast gives us in December and served at a single sitting.

Arrive from 7pm for a drink by the fire. Dinner begins at 7.30pm and runs for about three hours, with the kitchen bringing out each course themselves.
