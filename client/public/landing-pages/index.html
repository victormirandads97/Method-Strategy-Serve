The problem

Clicks are easy. Enquiries are the hard part.

You pay for ads, post on social and send people to your website. Then most of them leave without getting in touch. It's rarely the product. It's usually the page.

It loads too slowly

Most visits come from a phone, often on mobile data. Every second spent waiting is a reason to tap back.

Too many places to go

Menus, blog posts, About, Gallery. A visitor from your ad has to find the offer they clicked for. Many don't.

The offer isn't clear

If people can't tell what you do, who it's for and what it costs in a few seconds, they move on to someone who tells them.

No obvious next step

A phone number in the footer isn't a call to action. Without a clear button and a short form, interest goes nowhere.

The solution

One page. One offer. One next step.

A landing page is built for a single purpose: the campaign, service or event you're promoting right now. Everything on it moves the visitor towards one action, so more of the people you pay to reach actually get in touch.

Questions

The things people ask first.

How long does it take?

Most previews are ready within five working days of getting your answers. Once you approve, the page usually goes live within a couple of days. If you have a launch date, tell us in the form.

Where is the page hosted, and is there a monthly fee?

There's no monthly fee to us. We set up hosting on a fast global network in your own name. For a single page like this, that's usually free. If your setup would cost anything to run, you'll know before you approve.

How many changes can I ask for?

Two rounds of changes are included before launch, which is enough for almost everyone. After launch, small text changes in the first 30 days are free. Bigger changes are quoted first.

Who owns the page?

You do. The page, the words and the design are yours, and the domain and hosting sit in your name. If we stop working together, you keep everything.

When and how do I pay?

Nothing up front. Once you've seen your preview and approved it, we'll send an invoice for the agreed price, payable by card or bank transfer. The page goes live once it's paid.

I already have a website. Do I still need this?

Keep your website. A landing page sits alongside it for a specific campaign or offer, and you link to it from your ads, social profiles, Google Business Profile or a QR code. We can also link it from your main site.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, any photos you'd like to use, and answers to a short set of questions about the offer and your customers. If you're short on photos, we'll suggest options.
